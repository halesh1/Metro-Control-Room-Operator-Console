# User Research – Metro Control Room Operator Console

## 1. Introduction

User research was planned to understand the challenges faced by metro control room operators while monitoring train operations, managing technical faults and responding to passenger incidents.

The purpose is to identify user needs and design a centralized, efficient and easy-to-use Metro Control Room Operator Console.

## 2. Research Objectives

- Understand the daily responsibilities of metro control room operators.
- Identify difficulties in monitoring multiple trains.
- Understand how operators handle technical faults and alerts.
- Explore how passenger emergencies are reported and managed.
- Identify improvements needed in the existing control room workflow.
- Design an interface that supports faster and clearer decision-making.

## 3. Target Users

**Primary users:**
- Metro control room operators.
- Train monitoring supervisors.
- Operations coordinators.

**Secondary users:**
- Maintenance personnel.
- Emergency response teams.
- Station management staff.

## 4. User Interview Questions

The following questions can be used during user interviews:

1. What are the major responsibilities of a metro control room operator?
2. How do operators monitor multiple trains simultaneously?
3. What difficulties occur when a train is delayed?
4. How are technical faults identified and reported?
5. How are emergency situations communicated?
6. What challenges occur while coordinating passenger assistance?
7. What information should be immediately visible on the dashboard?
8. How can a centralized dashboard improve daily operations?
9. Which alerts require immediate attention?
10. What improvements would make the system easier to use?

## 6. Research Areas

### Train Monitoring
Operators need a clear view of train locations, speeds, destinations and operational conditions.

### Fault Management
Technical problems should be visible with their severity, affected train and current resolution status.

### Passenger Incident Management
Passenger incidents require clear reporting, assistance selection and status tracking.

### Emergency Coordination
Operators need a structured way to coordinate first aid, ambulance and emergency response assistance.

### Shift Management
Shift information and handover notes help maintain continuity between operators.

## 7. Initial Design Opportunities

Based on the project problem definition, the following opportunities were identified for exploration:

- Create a centralized operational dashboard.
- Provide real-time-style train status indicators using demonstration data.
- Organize fault alerts according to severity.
- Introduce a structured passenger incident workflow.
- Provide simulated emergency assistance dispatch options.
- Include shift management and handover information.

## 8. Expected Research Outcomes

The research aims to help identify:

- Important information required by operators.
- Common operational difficulties.
- Essential dashboard features.
- User expectations for emergency workflows.
- Opportunities to improve usability and information visibility.

## 9. Conclusion

User research provides the foundation for designing the Metro Control Room Operator Console. The identified research areas will guide the development of user personas, empathy maps, user journeys, wireframes and the final interactive prototype.
